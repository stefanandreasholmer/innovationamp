New to the standard?

ISO 56002, in a nutshell

ISO 56002 is the international guidance standard for innovation management systems — how an organization organizes leadership, strategy, support, and processes so innovation happens on purpose, not by luck. It's part of the ISO 56000 family: 56000 defines the fundamentals and vocabulary, 56002 describes how to run the system, and 56001 sets the certifiable requirements.

Not pursuing certification? You don't need to. Most organizations use 56002 as a reference model — a shared picture of what an innovation system looks like. InnovationAmp uses that as its measurement frame, so you get a structured health check whether an auditor has ever been involved or not.

The ISO 56000 family
56000 Fundamentals & vocabulary — the shared language
56001 Requirements — what auditors certify against
56002 Guidance for running the system — what InnovationAmp measures
5600X Deep dives — partnerships, IP, metrics, idea management

Certification lives in 56001; the day-to-day system lives in 56002. That's why we measure against 56002 — it's where practice happens.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is InnovationAmp an audit or certification tool?+

No — and it doesn't replace your certification body. It measures the layer an audit doesn't touch: whether people across the organization are aware of, and buy into, what the system prescribes.

Do we need to be ISO 56002 certified to use it?+

No. Items map to the standard's clauses, so it works whether you're certified, preparing for certification, or simply using 56002 as a reference model for your innovation system.

Why does this matter now?+

ISO 56002 has been guidance for a while. ISO 56001, published in 2024, is new: the first certifiable requirements standard for innovation management. That's created real pressure — more organizations pursuing certification, more scrutiny on whether the system underneath actually holds up. InnovationAmp sits one layer below certification: continuous sensing, not a once-a-year check.

How much time does a pulse take?+

About a minute per person. Short, recurring surveys instead of an annual assessment — designed to be answered honestly, not endured.

Who sees individual responses?+

No one. Results are reported in aggregate; splits show where a group disagrees — never who disagreed.

How is AI used?+

AmpAI turns aggregate scores and comments into theme summaries and recommendations, drawing on the innovation methods and case studies it was trained on. It interprets results — it doesn't make decisions for you.

What happens to our data?+

Responses are anonymous by design and stored per organization. Your data is never shared or sold, and it isn't used to train AI models for other customers.

What does it cost?+

Self-serve is $499 per organization per month — flat, regardless of size. Guided assessments with a results workshop are quoted per engagement.

Can consultants use InnovationAmp with their clients?+

Yes. It gives you a credible, ISO 56002-based diagnostic without building one from scratch, plus a way to keep measuring after the initial engagement ends. Get in touch and we'll set up a partner arrangement.

Will we be able to benchmark against other companies?+

It's on the roadmap: comparisons against organizations of similar size, industry, and geography, as the dataset grows.

Why I built this

Two decades of watching innovation systems from the inside, my own businesses and other people's, taught me the same lesson over and over. The paperwork gets built. Buy-in doesn't automatically follow. Not because anyone's lying, just because nobody's watching for it. Getting certified in ISO 56002 in 2021, I hoped the standard would close that gap. InnovationAmp tracks the part it misses, so you find the gap before it costs you something.
